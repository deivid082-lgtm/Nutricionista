# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JS with no build step, chosen by the user. Deployed on GitHub Pages from the `main` branch root (https://deivid082-lgtm.github.io/Nutricionista/).

## Users

Adults who want to eat better without stress. They arrive looking for simple, accessible recipes and practical nutrition guidance, and some of them book a consultation. Audience is general public, all ages and levels of cooking skill.

## Product Purpose

Personal-brand website of Miriam, technician in Dietetics and diploma holder in Nutrition and Dietetics from the Universidad de Barcelona (Instagram: @nutricionista_en_crisis). It promotes healthy eating, shares simple and accessible recipes, and converts visitors into consultation bookings. Success is people cooking the recipes and booking a consultation.

## Positioning

"Humor nutricional" voice backed by 20+ years in the collective-catering unit of a reference maternal and child hospital, where she writes menus considering pathologies and current nutrition guidelines. Passionate cook who invents her own recipes, now specializing in vegan food. Healthy food that nourishes and tastes good.

## Operating Context

Visitors mostly come from Instagram on a phone. Recipes are quick, low-ingredient and made in small kitchens. Consultations are offered individually, in person or online.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Recipe browsing with filters (breakfasts, snacks, for kids).
- Booking a consultation: individual consultation and online consultation. Booking is a guided 3-step form in a dialog, sent through Formspree (form ID still to be configured). Contact email: miriamorantos@gmail.com.
- Open decisions: Formspree form ID, privacy policy details (surname, NIF if needed) and real recipe content. The pediatric section was removed by the user.
- Recipe categories: Desayunos, Meriendas, Comidas. The "Hamburguesas de lentejas" recipe is an invented example.
- No services beyond individual and online consultation are confirmed. Collective-catering advisory is not offered through the site for now.

## Brand Commitments

Brand name: "Nutricionista en crisis". Tone: friendly, direct, with humor, no preaching. Existing identity is the Instagram profile (name, bio, food photography with warm plates). Chosen visual direction (user picked design 3 "Noche"): dark, photographic and minimalist. Full-bleed hero photo with a dark veil, Urbanist (thin 200 for headings, 400 for body), tangerine accent for key terms, deep green for the booking block. A sprout animation marks the vegan specialization.

## Evidence on Hand

- Her own photos and recipes (from Instagram), to be supplied; the site currently shows placeholders (`img/LEEME.txt` lists the nine needed).
- Recipe titles on the site are examples taken from her profile highlights and must be replaced by real ones.
- Credentials provided by the user (used verbatim in "Sobre mí"). Not available, and must not be invented: client testimonials, price list, registration number.

## Product Principles

1. Simple and accessible beats impressive: every recipe and tip must be usable by someone with little time and little cooking experience.
2. Humor lowers the guilt, never the rigor: advice stays professionally sound.
3. Real over invented: real photos, recipes and contact details only; mark anything unconfirmed.
4. One clear action: viewing recipes and booking a consultation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

General public of all ages. Target WCAG AA contrast, large readable type, full keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, and light and dark modes.
