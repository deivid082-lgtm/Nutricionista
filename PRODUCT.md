# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JS with no build step, chosen by the user. Deployed on GitHub Pages from the `main` branch root (https://deivid082-lgtm.github.io/Nutricionista/).

## Users

Adults who want to eat better without stress. They arrive looking for simple, accessible recipes and practical nutrition guidance, and some of them book a consultation. Audience is general public, all ages and levels of cooking skill.

## Product Purpose

Personal-brand website of Miriam, a dietitian-nutritionist (Instagram: @nutricionista_en_crisis). It promotes healthy eating, shares simple and accessible recipes, and converts visitors into consultation bookings. Success is people cooking the recipes and booking a consultation.

## Positioning

Dietitian-nutritionist voice with "humor nutricional": credible professional advice delivered without guilt or drama. Background in collective catering (restauración colectiva) and pediatric nutrition, and recipes developed in a small camper kitchen while travelling.

## Operating Context

Visitors mostly come from Instagram on a phone. Recipes are quick, low-ingredient and made in small kitchens. Consultations are offered individually, in person or online.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Recipe browsing with filters (breakfasts, snacks, for kids).
- Booking a consultation: individual consultation and online consultation. Booking is currently by email (`hola@tudominio.com` is a placeholder to be replaced); no backend or form handling exists.
- Open decisions: real contact email, real recipe pages and links, whether pediatric nutrition and collective-catering advisory stay prominent (the confirmed primary audience is adults).
- No services beyond individual and online consultation are confirmed. Collective-catering advisory is not offered through the site for now.

## Brand Commitments

Brand name: "Nutricionista en crisis". Tone: friendly, direct, with humor, no preaching. Existing identity is the Instagram profile (name, bio, food photography with warm plates). Colors and typography are not yet binding; the current site uses a coral accent with greenish-cool neutrals as a working choice only.

## Evidence on Hand

- Her own photos and recipes (from Instagram), to be supplied; the site currently shows placeholders (`img/LEEME.txt` lists the nine needed).
- Recipe titles on the site are examples taken from her profile highlights and must be replaced by real ones.
- Not available, and must not be invented: client testimonials, price list, professional credentials or registration number.

## Product Principles

1. Simple and accessible beats impressive: every recipe and tip must be usable by someone with little time and little cooking experience.
2. Humor lowers the guilt, never the rigor: advice stays professionally sound.
3. Real over invented: real photos, recipes and contact details only; mark anything unconfirmed.
4. One clear action: viewing recipes and booking a consultation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

General public of all ages. Target WCAG AA contrast, large readable type, full keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, and light and dark modes.
